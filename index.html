Security · Shipped v1.3

Porthawk

Go network scanning

A concurrent TCP port scanner with banner grabbing, written in Go with zero dependencies. Point it at your own network to find every listening service (a forgotten FTP daemon, a Redis bound to 0.0.0.0) and identify it from the banner it sends back. HTTPS ports get a real TLS handshake that records the certificate and cipher and flags expired certs, and -diff spots new or changed services week over week. v1.3 adds CI with race and fuzz testing, plus a -rate cap for gentle scans of fragile devices.

AI/ML · Shipped v0.2

Driftwatch

Python MLOps, drift detection

Models rarely fail loudly. They go quietly stale as production data drifts away from training data. Driftwatch fits a reference distribution once, then scores every new batch column by column with PSI, KS tests, Jensen-Shannon divergence and chi-square, flags correlation shifts, and tracks accuracy, log-loss or R² once labels arrive. A --fail-on flag turns drift or decay into a CI exit code, and everything runs offline.
